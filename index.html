The 3 Keys to Losing Fat, Not Muscle

Key 1
Optimised Protein Intake
When you're in a sustained calorie deficit, you're often eating less overall, which can mean less protein exactly when your body needs more. Research suggests 1.2-1.6g of protein per kg of bodyweight is needed to protect muscle during weight loss.
Key 2
Strategic Strength Training
Cardio is great for heart health, but it's resistance training that signals your body to preserve muscle. Even 2-3 sessions per week can make a significant difference, but the programme needs to be designed for your current fitness level and goals.
Key 3
Medical Supervision
A one-size-fits-all approach leaves you without the ongoing support you need to adjust as your body changes. Regular check-ins with a doctor who tracks body composition, not just the number on the scale, help ensure you're losing the right kind of weight.
